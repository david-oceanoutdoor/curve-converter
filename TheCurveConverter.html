The Curve Converter

Cuts an image or video into a tile grid and rebuilds it with the gaps removed or added. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Settings
For the 11px layout, set Horizontal gap 11, Last horizontal gap 12, Vertical gap 16 (gapped size 4010 × 2192).

Frame-accurate steps through the video one frame at a time and encodes to MP4 (H.264) with WebCodecs, so playback stutter can't affect the result. It's slower than real time, drops the audio, and needs an internet connection to load the MP4 muxer library. Set the fps to match your source. Real-time records playback live, keeps audio, but drops frames if your machine can't keep up. Keep this tab in the foreground either way. For masters, use the ffmpeg script or the After Effects scripts.
